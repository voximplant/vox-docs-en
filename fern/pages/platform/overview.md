# Communications Platform

Telecom used to mean metal boxes in basements, obscure protocols, and six-month carrier contracts. Voximplant turns all of that into clean, serverless JavaScript.

Voximplant is an enterprise-grade Communications Platform as a Service (CPaaS) that lets you control calls, video streams, messaging, and audio pipelines purely through code. With our serverless execution environment ([VoxEngine]()), your application logic runs directly alongside live audio streams in 15 global data centers—processing over 5 billion calls a year with sub-second latency.

## What's in the box

- **Serverless call control**: Write JavaScript scenarios to route, forward, bridge, record, or whisper into calls in real time. Zero servers to manage, infinite scale on demand.
- **Global telephony & SIP**: Spin up phone numbers in 100+ countries, connect existing SIP PBXs and SBCs, or hook directly into WebRTC and WhatsApp Business.
- **HD audio & video conferencing**: Multi-party conferences with up to 100 participants, spatial audio, screen sharing, and automatic mixing.
- **Intelligent speech processing**: Integrated multi-provider speech recognition (STT) and neural text-to-speech (TTS) for multi-language IVRs that actually understand what callers say.
- **Contact center & workflow automation**: Build full cloud contact center workflows with smart skills-based routing, queue management, agent desktop SDKs, and visual no-code tools.
- **Turnkey solutions**: Launch pre-built campaign dialers, two-factor voice verification, number masking, and appointment reminders in a fraction of the time.
