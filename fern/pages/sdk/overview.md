# Web/mobile SDKs

Turn any screen into a communication hub—without wrestling with raw WebRTC, signaling servers, or telecom headaches.

Voximplant’s client SDKs give you everything you need to embed real-time voice, video, and messaging directly into your apps. Whether you’re building a telehealth platform, a customer support widget, or the next big social app, you write the UI and business logic while we handle the media streams.

## What you can build

- **1-on-1 & group calls**: Crystal-clear peer-to-peer audio/video and multi-party conferences.
- **App-to-phone bridging**: Connect in-app users to real phone numbers (PSTN) and SIP networks seamlessly.
- **Production-ready call UX**: Built-in support for CallKit, PushKit, FCM push notifications, device switching, and background modes.
- **Smart audio**: Hardware-accelerated media handling and optional noise suppression out of the box.
